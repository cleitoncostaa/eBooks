# Content Policy

## Allowed

- Content authored by the repository owner.
- Public-domain content.
- Open-license content with attribution and license terms preserved.
- Content with explicit redistribution permission.

## Not Allowed

- Commercial books or workbooks without redistribution permission.
- Paid course material.
- Internal company documents.
- Personal notes containing private or sensitive data.
- Any file whose license cannot be verified.

## Review Process

Before adding a file:

1. Confirm the source.
2. Confirm the license.
3. Confirm redistribution is allowed.
4. Add source and license notes to the folder README.

If any step is unclear, do not publish the file in this repository.
